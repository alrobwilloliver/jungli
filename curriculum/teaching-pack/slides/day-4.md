# 01
## Skills & slash commands

---

<!-- header: 'Day 4 · The agent's powers + start your own' -->

## What's a skill?

- A **skill** = a packaged capability the agent can use — a **reusable recipe**
  (a review checklist, a build flow, a way of working).
- You invoke them with **slash commands** — type `/` to see what's there.

It's a **step-change:** you're *giving the agent new abilities*, not just asking nicely.

---

<!-- _class: demo -->

## Run a skill

I'll install **superpowers** and run one skill with you — watch
it bring a whole workflow, not just an answer.

Type `/` in the Code tab to see the skills and commands available.

---

<!-- _class: build -->

## Your turn: run one

Pick a skill and point it at **your own project**:

```text
/[skill-name] — then follow its prompts on my current project.
```

Notice what it does that a plain prompt wouldn't.

# 02
## MCP / integrations

---

## What's MCP?

- **MCP** = a standard way to plug the agent into **outside tools and data**:
  your files, a service, an app.
- It's how an agent reaches into your **real workflow**.

This is the doorway to the **Automation track** — same agent, new reach.

---

<!-- _class: demo -->

## Connect one

I'll connect **one** simple integration through the Claude app's
**connectors** — then ask the agent to actually *use* it.

Watch it reach outside the chat and bring something back.

---

<!-- _class: build -->

## Use it for something real

Once a tool is connected, put it to work:

```text
Using the [connected tool], find [X] and [do Y with it].
Show me what you used.
```

---

<!-- _class: trap -->

## Not magic

- A connected tool is powerful — but it's **not magic**.
- It's the **same agent loop** you already know, now with a **new tool** in reach.
- You still direct, review, and re-steer — exactly as before.

New powers, same habits.

# 03
## Scope your own project

---

## Pick a real use case

- Something from your **work or life** that you actually want.
- **Easiest path:** personalise **Build 3** — your career bot or your product bot.
- Or something new — your call.

The best project is one *you'd use on Monday*.

---

## Cut it down

- What's the **one useful thing** it does?
- Not *"an app like Uber"* → *"the one page that does the useful bit."*
- **Define "done"** in a single sentence.

Small and shipped beats big and stuck.

---

<!-- _class: build -->

## Scope it with the agent

Let Claude be your scoping partner:

```text
Here's my idea: [describe it]. Act as a scoping partner —
ask me questions first, then cut it down to the smallest
version I can build and deploy, and list the steps.
```

---

<!-- _class: build -->

## Start your project

1. Make a **new folder** for it (your own, like every build)
2. Write your one-line **"done"** at the top of a `CLAUDE.md`
3. Add your **rules and context**, then begin

Building continues in **open time** — lightly mentored.

---

- **Scope** a real project down to its smallest shippable version
- Started your **own build** — the one you came for
