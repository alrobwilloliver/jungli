# 01
## Planning from scratch

---

<!-- header: 'Day 5 · Ship, plan your next, demo' -->

## The repeatable method

The way to turn *any* idea into a shipped thing:

> **idea → one-line outcome → smallest shippable version →
> break into steps → build → deploy → improve**

Learn this once, use it forever. It's the real capstone.

---

## Plan *with* the agent

- Don't ask it to build **blind** — **plan first**.
- **Brainstorm together:** talk the idea through before any code.
- Read the plan, steer it, *then* build.

The agent is a great planning partner — use it as one.

---

<!-- _class: trap -->

## Gold-plating kills shipping

- **Scoping is 80% of success.** Getting it small is the hard part.
- **Gold-plating** — polishing what nobody asked for — is why projects stall.
- Ship the small version. **Improve it once it's real.**

Small and shipped beats big and stuck.

---

<!-- _class: build -->

## Plan your next project

The one you'll build *after* you go home:

```text
I want to build [X]. Act as my planning partner: ask me
questions, then help me write a one-page plan — the smallest
first version, then a list of steps to get there.
```

Leave today with a **one-page plan** in hand.

# 02
## Demos & peer testing

---

## What to show

Keep it **short and kind** — unfinished is fine, that's the point:

- What it **does**
- Who it's **for**
- What's still **fragile**
- What's **next**

---

<!-- _class: build -->

## Demo, then test

1. **Demo** your build to the group
2. **Test two** other people's — actually use them
3. Give **one** useful piece of feedback each

Using each other's builds is the fastest way to see what's real.
