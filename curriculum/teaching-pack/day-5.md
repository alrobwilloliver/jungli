# Day 5 — Ship, plan your next, demo

*~2 hours structured · land the plane. Goal: ship what they've got, teach the
repeatable way to plan a project, demo, and send them off able to keep building.*

---

## Lesson 5.1 — Planning a project from scratch *(concept, ~30 min)*

*The capstone skill — so they can keep building their own things after the residency.*

**Talking points:**
- The repeatable method: **idea → one-line outcome → smallest shippable version →
  break into steps → build → deploy → improve.**
- **Brainstorm *with* the agent** — don't ask it to build blind; plan first.
- **Scoping is 80% of success;** gold-plating is the enemy of shipping.
- A reusable **checklist** they take home and use on any future idea.

**Resources:** the one-page "project planner" (the steps above) · *(optional)* the
brainstorming skill.

**Practical task:** write a one-page plan for your **next** project (the one you'll
build after you go home).

**Questions:** ① What are the steps of the method? ② What's the most common reason
projects stall? ③ What's your next project's one-line outcome?

**Base prompt:**
```
I want to build [X]. Act as my planning partner: ask me questions, then help me
write a one-page plan — the smallest first version, then a list of steps to get there.
```

---

## Lesson 5.2 — Demos & peer testing *(~40 min)*

**Talking points:**
- **What to show:** what it does · who it's for · what's still fragile · what's next.
- Keep it short and kind. Unfinished is fine — that's the point.
- **Peer testing:** actually use each other's builds — it's the fastest way to see
  what's genuinely usable.

**Resources:** the group · a simple demo order · everyone's live URLs.

**Practical task:** demo your build; then test two other people's and give one
piece of useful feedback each.

**Questions (reflection):** ① What surprised you in someone else's build? ② What's
the next thing you'd add to yours?

---

## Lesson 5.3 — The territory map + keep going *(~30 min)*

**Talking points:**
- **What you now own (the map):** prompting · tokens & context · git & deploy ·
  apps · API keys · one skill · one MCP.
- **What's out there next:** advanced MCP · **building your own skills** · subagents
  · automation/scheduling · the Agent SDK.
- The **"agent as teammate" paradigm** (Karpathy's third shift — the LLM as a
  persistent teammate, not a website or an app) as where this all heads.
- **Keep going:** managing usage/cost (model choice, `/clear`) · where to get
  unstuck (docs, communities) · your next project.

**Resources:** [`prep/claude-plans.md`](../../prep/claude-plans.md) ·
`code.claude.com/docs` · a short "what next" links list.

**Practical task:** commit to a next project + one resource you'll use to keep
learning.

**Questions:** ① Name one thing you can build now that you couldn't on Day 1.
② What's your very next step?

---

**End of Day 5 — students leave with:** a shipped project, a one-page plan for
their next one, the map of what's beyond, and genuine confidence driving Claude Code.
