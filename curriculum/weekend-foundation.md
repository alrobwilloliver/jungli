# Weekend Foundation — 2-Day Taster Syllabus

*Jungli AI Learning Residency · Dandeli, Karnataka · Lead instructor: Alan Oliver*

## The taster in one paragraph

Two days to go from *"I've never built anything"* to a **real page live on the
internet** and your **first taste of giving an AI a memory**. No coding, no
terminal, no prior experience — this is the ground floor. It stands completely
on its own: come for the weekend, leave with something you made and the
confidence that you can do this. And if it lights you up, it rolls **straight
into the 5-day course** — the same two moves come back, on bigger builds.

## Who this is for

- Total beginners who use AI casually (ChatGPT/Claude for prompts and content)
  and want to *make* something, not just chat.
- **Weekend-first people** — the most common thing our survey respondents said
  they'd actually commit to is "I'd come for a weekend first." This is that.
- Anyone who quietly worries they're **"not technical enough."** You are exactly
  who this is built for. We name that fear on Day 1 and put it to bed.

**No coding experience required.** If you've never opened a terminal, good — you
won't need to.

## What you'll leave with

1. **A live landing page** — your name and a few words about you, on a real URL
   you can text to a friend.
2. **A "second brain" starter** — a small folder of your own notes that Claude
   can read, organise, and answer questions from.
3. **The core mental model** — what Claude Code actually is, and the one loop you
   use for everything: *ask → it builds → you look → you change it.*
4. **A running start** — if you continue into the 5-day course, you arrive
   already fluent in the basics.

## The shape of the weekend

| Day | When | Theme | You leave with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sat** | Full day (most mentored) | **Zero → a live site** | A personal landing page, live on the internet |
| **Sun** | Shared cross-track day (partial instructor time) | **Give Claude a memory** | A second-brain starter the agent answers from |

The weekend is a deliberate pair: **Saturday teaches *output*** — make a thing,
put it in the world. **Sunday teaches *input*** — how you give an AI a memory by
handing it your own files. Together they're the two halves of building with AI.

## Day 1 — Zero → a live website *(Saturday)*

**Goal:** Everyone ends the day with a website **live on the internet**. No
exceptions. This is the confidence win the whole weekend is built on.

**We'll teach (short, ~30%):**
- What an LLM and an "AI agent" actually are — without the mysticism.
- What Claude Code is and how it works: it reads and writes files on your
  computer, runs commands, makes a plan, tries things, and sometimes fails — and
  that's normal.
- The core loop you'll use all weekend: **ask → it builds → you look → you change it.**
- What a "token" is, lightly — so the app's limits make sense later.
- What git and GitHub are, gently: **save points** for your work, and the way to
  put it on the internet.

**We'll build (the rest of the day):**
- Use Claude Code to build a **basic personal landing page** — your name, a
  tagline, a couple of sections — starting from a ready, prompt-driven template.
- Save it to GitHub and deploy it to **Vercel** to get a live URL.
- Text the link to a friend. *That's the moment.*

**Leave with:** a live landing page + the realisation that you can make the
internet do things.

> **Lighter than the course.** The 5-day course's Day 1 builds a richer version
> of this — with a working email-capture form. Here we keep it simple: the win is
> *"it's live and it's mine."*

## Day 2 — Give Claude a memory *(Sunday)*

**Goal:** The *"whoa — it actually knows my stuff"* moment. Build a **second
brain**: a small set of notes the agent reads and answers from. This is how you
*feel* context and memory — the single idea everything else in AI is built on.

**We'll teach:**
- **Context & memory:** what the agent can and can't "see," and why giving it the
  right files is the whole game.
- **Standing instructions:** how to give Claude Code notes it reads *every time*,
  so it remembers how you like to work.
- Why this matters: an agent is really just **a model + the context you give it.**
  Get good at context and you can build almost anything.

**We'll build (from a ready starter):**
- A small folder of **markdown notes about you** — a short bio, a few interests
  or projects — tidied and linked by Claude Code.
- Then **ask it real questions** and watch it answer *only* from what you gave it.

**Optional — see it, then grow it** *(the "extra" track for a light-instructor
day; right-sizes a session that can otherwise finish fast):*
- Open your vault in **Obsidian** (a free notes app) and turn on **graph view** —
  your notes become dots, your `[[links]]` become lines. You can *see* your brain.
- Then make it richer: add a few more linked notes, refresh the index, give Claude
  a short standing-instructions file, and ask harder questions across notes.
- Full guide: [`../projects/second-brain/OBSIDIAN.md`](../projects/second-brain/OBSIDIAN.md).

**Leave with:** your own second-brain starter + the most important idea in the
whole track — and, if you're continuing, the exact thing the course's Day 2
expands into a full, linked vault.

## How the weekend is scheduled

Part of a **blended three-track residency**, so the two days have different
rhythms:

- **Saturday = the anchored build day.** The most instructor time of the weekend.
  A short teach up front, then hands-on building with the instructor moving around
  the room. Nobody leaves without a live URL.
- **Sunday = a shared cross-track day** with **partial instructor time** — so it's
  built to run from a **ready starter**, not open-ended. You're guided, paired, and
  the scaffolding does the heavy lifting where the instructor can't be everywhere.

Roughly **30% teaching / 70% building** across both days.

## The 7-day path — how this connects to the course

The weekend is complete on its own. It's also the **on-ramp to the 5-day course**:

- **Do just the weekend (2 days):** you leave with a live page, a second-brain
  starter, and real confidence. A whole experience.
- **Continue into the course (5 more days = 7 total):** Days 1–2 of the course
  revisit these two moves on **richer builds** — a landing page with email
  capture, and a full linked second brain — then go deeper: a chat agent grounded
  in your docs, your own project built 1:1, and shipping it. See
  [`ai-agents-track.md`](ai-agents-track.md).

For a 7-day learner this overlap is **reinforcement, not repetition**: you meet
the core moves twice, going deeper each time — exactly the pace beginners need.

## Facilitator notes (Alan)

- **Protect the Saturday win.** If anyone leaves Day 1 without a live URL, the
  weekend hasn't landed. Pre-arrival setup (see [`../prep/`](../prep/)) exists so
  Saturday isn't lost to installs.
- **Name the "not technical enough" fear on Day 1** — it's real for ~1 in 3
  learners (per the survey). Normalising it early is half the battle.
- **Sunday runs from a scaffolded starter.** With only partial instructor time,
  the second-brain-lite build must be guided and pre-built enough that pairs can
  progress without you at their shoulder. The optional **Obsidian + grow-your-brain**
  track (see Day 2) is the pacing buffer — it absorbs faster finishers so you can
  spend your limited time with whoever's stuck.
- **Keep it non-sensitive.** Learners use their *own* light notes or the sample
  persona — never paste real CVs, contact details, or secrets into anything that
  gets committed.
- **Sell the continuation honestly.** The weekend is genuinely whole; the course
  is *more*, not the "real" version they missed. Both are real.
