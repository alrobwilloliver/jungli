# Before You Arrive — Prerequisites

*Read this and complete the checklist **before** you travel. Day 1 is where you
ship your first website — we don't want to lose it to installs and downloads on
the jungle wifi. Do all of this at home, on good internet.*

> This page has a **shared baseline** for every resident (all three tracks), then a
> section specific to the **AI & Agents** track. If you're on the Creative or
> Automation track, your instructor will give you that track's extra steps.

---

## Part 1 — Everyone (all tracks)

### Bring
- **A laptop** — macOS 13+ or Windows 10/11. (Not a tablet or Chromebook.)
- **Your charger** + a **universal/India power adapter** (Type D / M sockets).
- **4 GB+ RAM** and a few GB of free disk space.
- Headphones (handy for video walkthroughs).

### Expect
- **Rural connectivity.** Internet works but can be patchy. Do all downloads,
  installs, and account sign-ups **at home, on good wifi, before you come.**

### Accounts everyone needs
1. **A Claude account with a paid plan.** ⚠️ The **free** Claude plan does **not**
   include the coding features we use all week. You need at least **Claude Pro**.
   See [`claude-plans.md`](claude-plans.md) to choose a plan (and what to do if
   you're worried about cost).
2. **An email you can access on your laptop** (for sign-in links).

## Part 2 — AI & Agents track (Alan's track)

We build using the **Claude desktop app** — the friendly version with buttons, not
the terminal. You'll set up four things before arrival. Pick your guide:

- 🍎 **On a Mac?** → [`setup-mac.md`](setup-mac.md)
- 🪟 **On Windows?** → [`setup-windows.md`](setup-windows.md)

Both guides walk you through:
1. **The Claude desktop app** — this is the tool we build with. (It already
   includes "Claude Code" — you don't install anything extra.)
2. **Node.js** — one free tool that lets the websites/apps you build actually run.
3. **Git + a GitHub account** — your "save points" and where your work goes online.
4. **A Vercel account** — where you'll publish your site to a real, live URL.

Each guide ends by having you **paste one setup prompt into the Claude app**
([`setup-prompt.md`](setup-prompt.md)) — Claude then checks everything is working
and creates your first test file. The agent does the fiddly bits for you.

### Pre-arrival check ✅
You're ready for Day 1 when:

- [ ] The **Claude app** opens and you can sign in (with your **paid** plan)
- [ ] You can open the **Code** tab in the app (it won't ask you to upgrade)
- [ ] **Node.js** is installed (the setup prompt confirms this)
- [ ] You can sign in to **github.com** and **vercel.com**
- [ ] You ran the **setup prompt** and Claude created your test file

### If you get stuck
Don't worry — bring it to Day 1 and we'll sort it in the first hour. But please
*try* the steps first. Note any error messages (a screenshot is perfect).
