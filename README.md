# Jungli AI Learning Residency — AI & Agents Track

Teaching materials for the **AI & Agents** track of the Jungli AI Learning
Residency (Dandeli, Karnataka). Five days to take complete beginners from
**zero to building real software with Claude Code** — leaving with a live
website, a working app, and a project of their own.

> One of three tracks at the residency. The others — **Creative** (AI video &
> content) and **Automation** (workflows) — are run by other instructors. This
> repo is **Alan Oliver's** track.

## Who this is for

You use ChatGPT or Claude already — for writing, research, quick answers — and
you want to actually *build* something with AI. A website. A tool. A bot.
**You don't need to know how to code.** If you've never opened a terminal,
you're exactly who this is designed for.

## Two formats

- **5-day sprint (Mon→Fri):** the full programme. Mon–Wed are structured morning sessions; Thursday is a cross-track day where all three tracks run simultaneously and learners work on their own projects; Friday morning is the final ship-and-demo session.
- **Weekend taster (Sat+Sun):** Sessions 1–2 in compressed form — enough to leave with a live site and the core Claude Code skills. Saturday full day; Sunday is a shared cross-track day. A lower-stakes entry point that feeds into the next 5-day cohort.

## What you'll leave with

1. A **live landing page** with a real email-capture form (shipped Day 1).
2. A **"second brain"** — your notes or product info structured for an AI to use.
3. A **chat agent grounded in your docs** — a career bot or a product Q&A bot.
4. **Your own project** — something useful for your work or life, started on
   Thursday in self-directed build time and shipped on Friday.
5. Real **confidence driving Claude Code**: brief it, plan with it, debug it,
   ship with it — on your own, after you leave.
